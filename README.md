YOU NEED A REALITY CHECK is a no-fluff survival guide for Gen Z and young adults trapped between consuming content about adulthood and actually living it.

The internet can teach you how to build a career, find love, get rich, become productive, and "fix your life" before you have even left the couch. You can spend hours watching, comparing, researching and planning while real life quietly moves on.

This book challenges three seductive myths: that career success automatically follows passion, that love should be effortless, and that family can fund your self-discovery indefinitely. Reality works differently. Careers require useful skills. Relationships require compatibility and effort. Money requires earning, saving and judgment. Adulthood requires becoming an asset rather than a liability.

Across 30 concise concepts in five parts, you'll learn to navigate:

The Scoreboard: Incentives, power and human behaviour.
The Shortcuts: Cognitive biases and self-deception.
The Machine: Money, attention, algorithms, institutions and influence.
The Shift: AI, demographic change and geopolitical disruption.
The Playbook: Risk, leverage, networks, compounding and action.
The book also explores digital wellness, social media addiction, screen time, the attention economy, internet culture, dating, entertainment, money and family economics.

Every concept ends with a practical field assignment designed to move you from consuming information to taking action.

No hustle-culture fantasies. No endless theory. No ideological comfort.

Just one uncompromising idea:

Reality is unforgiving, but it is where everything real gets built.

At 63,700+ words, You Need a Reality Check is more than a read: it's a reference. It is built to be consulted when you lose your way and applied immediately when you find your footing.

Stop consuming adulthood. Start living it.
